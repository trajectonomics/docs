---
title: balance
description: Show every account's balance and net worth at a date.
---

`traj balance` shows every account's balance and net worth at a date you name; the CLI reads no
clock
([ADR 0029](https://github.com/trajectonomics/cli/blob/v0.1.0/docs/adr/0029-reads-take-their-date-from-the-caller.md)).

- `--as-of <string>` — the date the balances are stated at, as `YYYY-MM-DD`; required
- `--trial-balance` — add the Trial Balance and assert its debit and credit totals agree

```bash
traj balance --as-of 2026-03-31 --json           # Every account's balance and net worth at one date.
traj balance --as-of 2026-03-31 --trial-balance  # Add the trial balance and check that debits equal credits.
```

See [Commands](/docs/cli/commands/) for the global options, exit codes and error envelope.
