`traj baseline` shows the Baseline a Projection starts from. It is derived on demand from the stored
rows and refuses what it cannot state
([ADR 0026](https://github.com/trajectonomics/cli/blob/v0.2.0/docs/adr/0026-baseline-is-derived-and-balance-sheet-only.md)).

## What a Baseline holds

Only asset and liability leaf accounts cross, and only when their balance at the date is non-zero.
Equity, income and expense never do: equity is the contra side of an opening balance, and income and
expense are flows. A header account never appears either, while an account closed with
`traj account close` does, because closing keeps its history. Net worth is assets minus liabilities,
and it counts nothing else.

## `traj baseline show`

Show the Baseline a Projection starts from at a date.

- `--as-of <string>` — the date the Baseline is stated at, as `YYYY-MM-DD`; required

```bash
traj baseline show --as-of 2026-03-31 --json  # The Baseline a Projection would start from at one date.
```

On the [Quickstart](/docs/cli/)'s funded ledger the Baseline is one row: `Cash`, an asset, `879.50`
THB. The equity account `Opening` and the expense account `Food` do not cross.

## When a Baseline refuses

A balance with no representation refuses the whole Baseline at exit code `3` rather than dropping or
clamping the account. An overdrawn asset and an overpaid loan are both negative once stated, so
carrying either would make the Baseline disagree with the ledger without saying so.

On the Quickstart's ledger without its opening balance, Cash holds `-120.50` THB and the command
fails with `NEGATIVE_AMOUNT` and `Cash holds -120.50 THB, which is negative once stated as an
asset`. The way to a Baseline that succeeds is a funded ledger: create the equity account, then post
the [opening balance](/docs/cli/commands/tx/) that keeps every asset and liability positive.
